## Assumptions

- Bar customers pay via M-Pesa or cash only (no cards)
- First client is single bar location, not a chain
- Staff takes orders (not customer self-ordering via phone)
- Kenyan bar owners check WhatsApp daily, not email
- M-Pesa STK Push works reliably for 95%+ of transactions
- Supabase free tier (500MB) sufficient for 2+ years with 10 active stores
- Render free tier sufficient for MVP (1 bar client)
- Happy hour pricing is simple time-based discount
- Pre-loaded menu templates work for most Kenyan bars
- WhatsApp daily summary sufficient for EOD reporting
